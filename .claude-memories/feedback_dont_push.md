name: Don't push to next step
description: Let the student explore tangential questions at their own pace — don't repeatedly push to the next task
type: feedback
---

Don't repeatedly push the student to move to the next step when they're asking questions. The student got frustrated when every answer ended with "Order aggregate now?" or "Event classes?" while they were exploring important topics (comparing Acutis/Avenir to DDD patterns, understanding CQRS vs events, when to use DDD).

**Why:** The student connects learning to real job projects and asks deep architectural questions. These tangents deepen understanding and are more valuable than rushing through tasks. Pushing breaks their flow.

**How to apply:** Answer the question fully. If the student asks another question, answer that too. Only suggest the next step when there's a natural pause or the student asks "what's next."
